# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 2026-03-23

## Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how our web application "CryptoDash" handles user information. The Service is a monitoring-only cryptocurrency dashboard designed to provide users with market data and insights without requiring the collection or storage of personal information.

## Information Collection

We do **not** collect, store, or process any personal data from users.

* No account registration is required
* No personal identifiers (such as name, email, or address) are collected
* No transaction or wallet data is stored

## Use of Data

As we do not collect any user data, we do not use, analyze, or share personal information for any purpose.

## Cookies and Tracking

The Service does not use cookies, tracking technologies, or analytics tools to monitor user activity.

## Data Sharing and Selling

We do **not** sell, rent, or share user data with third parties, as no user data is collected.

## Third-Party Services

The Service may display publicly available cryptocurrency market data sourced from third-party providers. These providers may have their own privacy policies, and we encourage users to review them independently.

## Data Security

Since we do not collect or store user data, there is no personal information at risk of unauthorized access or misuse through the Service.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
